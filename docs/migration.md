The three newer stories about Iceland, Antarctica, and the Wonders of the World are now recovered from exact snapshots provided by Jupiter and included in the journal, homepage, RSS, and sitemap. The Indian festivals article still awaits recovery.

The optional `scripts/recover-archive.py` uses Python and BeautifulSoup, writes raw recovery data to `src/data/recovered.json`, and caches downloaded HTML in `/tmp/jupiter-recovery`. It never downloads photographs or overwrites curated Markdown. Network access and archive availability are needed to rerun it.
