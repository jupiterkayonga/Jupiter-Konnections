<p class="eyebrow">Rajasthan, India · Canon 5D Mark IV · Portrait collection</p>

<p>Faces of India is a portrait collection from my journey across India: everyday scenes, festivals, wedding processions and the people I met along the way.</p>
<p>Travelling in India gave me opportunities to learn about myself and different aspects of Indian culture. This series celebrates the people who welcomed me, their hospitality, and the encounters that made the journey memorable. I hope it inspires you to experience that warmth for yourself.</p>
