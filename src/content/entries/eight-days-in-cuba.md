<p>In 2015, my journey through Cuba included an eight-day trip with <a href="https://www.cubagrouptour.com/tour/8-day-cuba-tour-package" rel="noopener noreferrer">Cuban Adventures</a>. This is the wider journey behind my Havana story, the classic cars of Almendrones, and the boxing and baseball photographs in Cuban Passion.</p>
<p>From city streets to red-earth paths, the trip took me beyond a single view of Cuba. Alongside the landscapes, my camera recorded meals, fellow travellers and encounters that became stories of their own.</p>

<h2>Travelling with Cuban Adventures</h2>
<p>I travelled with Cuban Adventures on their eight-day tour in 2015. The photographs below follow the parts of that journey I have brought together here: Havana, Viñales and Trinidad.</p>
<p>If you are considering a similar trip, the company’s <a href="https://www.cubagrouptour.com/tour/8-day-cuba-tour-package" rel="noopener noreferrer">current eight-day itinerary</a> is the place to check today’s route and booking details. My account looks back at the trip I took in 2015.</p>

<h2>Havana: where the stories began</h2>
<p>I arrived in Havana in Alejandro’s old American taxi and checked into a casa particular. After a long journey, I left exploring until the following morning. The next day brought an encounter I could not have planned: an injury during a run, followed by the kindness of Eva, Isabelle and Martha.</p>
<p>That story deserves its own space. In <a href="/todo-bien-en-habana/">Todo bien en Habana!</a>, I tell how an interrupted morning became a friendship. It is one of the personal threads running through these photographs.</p>

![A view across Havana’s rooftops beneath a blue sky.](../../assets/images/cuba-journey-havana-rooftops.jpg)

<p>The cars also became a collection of their own. Rather than repeat that whole series here, <a href="/portfolio/almendrones/">Almendrones</a> gathers the colours, shapes and details I photographed across Cuba.</p>

![Musicians perform with percussion instruments during an evening in Havana.](../../assets/images/cuba-journey-havana-musicians.jpg)

<h2>Viñales: beyond the city</h2>

![Green fields and limestone hills stretch across the countryside around Viñales.](../../assets/images/cuba-journey-vinales-cover.jpg)

<p>After Havana, I spent a few days in Viñales. My photographs move from streets and rooftops to fields, red-earth paths and the surrounding hills. I photographed a farmer with his oxen, visited the tobacco-growing countryside and took snapshots with the people travelling alongside me.</p>

![A red-earth path winds through the green Cuban countryside.](../../assets/images/cuba-journey-countryside-path.jpg)
![A Cuban farmer stands beside a pair of oxen.](../../assets/images/cuba-journey-farmer-oxen.jpg)

![Jupiter during a tobacco-related visit in the Cuban countryside.](../../assets/images/cuba-journey-tobacco-visit.jpg)
![Jupiter poses with two fellow travellers outside a house in Cuba.](../../assets/images/cuba-journey-travellers.jpg)

<p>Viñales was also where I attended my first baseball game. Earlier in Havana, I had watched a training session at the Rafael Trejo boxing gym. Both encounters now sit together in <a href="/portfolio/cuban-sports/">Cuban Passion</a>, a photo essay about the sporting life I encountered on this trip.</p>

![Low houses and washing lines beside a path in the Cuban countryside.](../../assets/images/cuba-journey-country-houses.jpg)
